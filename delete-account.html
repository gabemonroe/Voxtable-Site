Request VoxTable account deletion

Important: The deletion workflow is being finalized and has not yet been verified for public use. Sending a request does not automatically delete your account.

Contact the operator

To ask about deleting your VoxTable account, email gabemonroe103189@gmail.com with the subject “VoxTable Account Deletion Request.” Use the email address associated with your VoxTable account where possible. Do not send passwords, login tokens, or identity documents in the initial request.

The operator will need to verify account ownership, determine which authentication/profile records can be removed, and explain any records that must be retained for security or legal reasons. No automatic processing or response time is promised.

What about local matches and rosters?

VoxTable match history, scores, and rosters are stored locally by default. Account deletion may not remove copies stored on your device or files you exported. Removing app data may erase local records permanently, so preserve any exports you want to keep before changing device storage.

Before public release

The operator must test the account-deletion process end to end, confirm which account data is deleted or retained, and update this page with accurate processing details. This page is currently informational and should not be treated as proof of a functioning deletion service.
