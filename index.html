Keep the game moving.

VoxTable is an Android tabletop match companion with scoring, chess clocks, saved army rosters, match history and offline Guest mode.

Beta information

VoxTable is preparing for Google Play closed testing. Availability and enrollment are managed through the official Google Play test invitation when released. This site does not provide a public installation or enrollment link yet.

Need help? Contact support. Review our privacy policy or account deletion information.

Independent companion

VoxTable is an independent tabletop utility. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Games Workshop.
