VoxTable Privacy Policy

Draft for owner review · Updated October 13, 2026

This policy describes the current beta design. The operator must confirm actual production configuration and provider practices before public release.

What the app handles

VoxTable stores match information, scoring, clocks, army rosters, and battle history on your device. Guest mode is designed to work offline. You may import or export roster files using Android's document picker and sharing features; exported files and copies you share may be accessible to their recipients.

Optional Google sign-in uses Google authentication and Supabase to manage account authentication and sessions. This may involve account identifiers such as your email address, authentication tokens, and technical connection or security records processed by those services. Google and Supabase process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

How information is used

Local match and roster information supports the app's tabletop functions. Authentication information supports optional sign-in and account/session management. Information you voluntarily send to support is used to respond to your request. VoxTable is not currently intended to synchronize gameplay records to a cloud account, display advertisements, or process in-app purchases.

Storage and retention

Local records remain on your device unless you delete them or remove app data. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app may permanently erase local records. Authentication-related information may be retained by service providers subject to their policies and applicable requirements. A specific retention period has not yet been established for support correspondence; the operator will review this before launch.

Sharing and security

Authentication involves Google and Supabase. Files are shared only when you initiate import/export or sharing. No system is guaranteed completely secure. Do not include passwords or sensitive credentials in support messages.

Your choices

You can use Guest mode without Google sign-in. You can request help with account deletion through the account deletion page. Account deletion is not yet confirmed operational for public release, and this page does not represent that a deletion has been completed.

Children and changes

VoxTable is designed as a tabletop utility, not specifically for children. The operator must finalize target-audience and age-related disclosures before Google Play distribution. This policy may change as the beta is prepared; updates will be posted here.
